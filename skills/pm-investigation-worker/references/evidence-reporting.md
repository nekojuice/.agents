# Evidence Reporting

For every material claim, separate:

1. **Observation** - what the source directly contains.
2. **Inference** - what the observation suggests.
3. **Confidence** - low, medium, or high.
4. **Qualification** - what could make the conclusion incomplete or wrong.

Use direct paths and symbols where possible. Do not paste large source passages.

Use these status meanings:

- `answered`: authoritative evidence resolves the question.
- `confirmed`: evidence directly supports the suspected condition or cause.
- `suspected`: evidence supports a hypothesis but does not prove it.
- `not-reproduced`: no permitted evidence reproduced the reported behavior; this does not mean invalid.
- `needs-input`: missing scope, authority, environment, or a required operation blocked by isolation.

Factory candidates are suggestions to the PM, never approvals or implementation plans.
