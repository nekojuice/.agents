# Case Classification

## Question

The user needs an explanation, status, usage guidance, or factual answer. If a supported answer resolves the concern, close it as answered without factory work.

## Suspected bug

Observed or reported behavior may violate an expectation, but either the expectation or the behavior is not sufficiently verified.

## Confirmed bug

Evidence establishes both the accepted expectation and the violating behavior or implementation path.

## Design concern

The system may behave as implemented, but the design creates usability, correctness, maintenance, security, or operational harm.

## Initiative

A desired capability or project outcome that is not primarily a defect correction.

## Risk

A condition that could harm delivery or operation but is not yet an active defect or approved initiative.

Do not force ambiguous inputs into a definitive class. Keep them investigating and state what evidence would resolve the classification.
