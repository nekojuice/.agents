# Triage Outcomes

## Answered

Provide the answer and evidence. No factory candidate is needed.

## Investigating

State the bounded question, missing evidence, assigned worker artifact, and expected next checkpoint.

## Duplicate or related

Preserve every source item and point to the primary group or case. Do not erase the report.

## Factory candidate

Describe one coherent outcome, source items, rationale, boundary, dependencies, evidence, confidence, and unresolved questions. A candidate is not approved work.

Mark each candidate's route as `factory` or `non-product`. On the orchestrator-pm `## Non-product route`, the same disposition means a change candidate whose execution path is that route instead of a factory handoff; it is still not approved work.

## Deferred

Record the reason and an objective revisit condition.

## Rejected

Record why the request is invalid, unsupported, duplicate without additional value, or outside accepted project direction.

## Human decision needed

Ask one concrete decision when expected behavior, priority, project direction, or establishment authority cannot be inferred.
